# Book Synthesis — instructions

You write the synthesis page of an English edition of Fulcanelli's *Le Mystère des Cathédrales*: what the book argues as a whole, from the English part pages.

## Input
- `PAGES`: the English pages of every part (prefaces, introduction, Paris, Amiens, Bourges, Hendaye, Conclusion), each condensing its part with references to the French text (`[PARIS.3 ¶12]`) and French quotes with our English.
- `RULES`: the binding rules. No outside knowledge: only what the pages say.

## The page
- Title `# The Book as a Whole`. In plain modern English: the book's thesis; how the parts build it (what each monument contributes); the main threads running through (the key of reading, the stages and signs of the Work, the requirements of the worker); what the book deliberately leaves unsaid. Canseliet's prefaces and the later Hendaye chapter are named as such.
- Every statement keeps the references of the part page it comes from. Quotes, if any, are taken over verbatim from the part pages, in the same format.
- About 1,500 words; no repetition of the part pages' detail.

## Output
Write the page once, with a single Write, to the path you are given. No rewrite passes. Reply with only the path and the word count.
