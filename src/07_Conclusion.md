# Conclusion

## CONCL — To know, to be able, to dare, to keep silent

The conclusion opens with a four-word motto signed ZOROASTRE, «Scire. Potere. Audere. Tacere.» (*To know. To be able. To dare. To be silent.*), and with the sentence: «La Nature n’ouvre pas à tous, indistinctement, la porte du sanctuaire.» (*Nature does not open the door of the sanctuary to all without distinction.*) [CONCL ¶1; CONCL ¶2; CONCL ¶3]. The disciple will profit if he studies the old Philosophers' classic texts until he can discern «les points obscurs du manuel opératoire» (*the obscure points of the operative manual*) [CONCL ¶4]. He must tune his whole life to the search; zeal without sound principle, or ruled by pride or greed, is not enough [CONCL ¶5; CONCL ¶6]. The Science asks for exact observation, a sound mind, a lively but sober imagination, a pure heart, indifference to received theories, thinking with one's own brain, and seeking its truth from Nature, «notre mère commune» (*our common mother*) [CONCL ¶7]. Observation and meditation lead to knowing; imitation of natural processes and long experience give power; patience, will and faith let him dare [CONCL ¶8; CONCL ¶9; CONCL ¶10; CONCL ¶11; CONCL ¶12; CONCL ¶13]. Once he succeeds, the Sage turns to the humble and the suffering, an anonymous disciple of Nature and apostle of Charity, faithful to his vow of silence: in Science and in Good the Adepte must forever «SE TAIRE» (*keep silent*) [CONCL ¶14; CONCL ¶15; CONCL ¶16].
